The [licence](LICENSE) covers the code. It does not cover the name or the mark,
and this file is the difference.

- The name **Aera**, and **Aera Lend**
- The Aera lockup and the droplet mark
- The lime `#b1fa0c` as used with either of the above

None of these appear in this repository. They are named here so the boundary is
explicit rather than assumed.

## What you may do

Everything the licence permits, and:

- **Say what your work is.** "A liquidation bot for Aera", "an interface to the
  Aera protocol", "a fork of Aera Protocol v1" — accurate, descriptive
  statements of fact are nominative use and need no permission.
- **Publish research.** Name Aera in a security disclosure, a benchmark, a post
  or a paper. Please do.
- **Fork the code**, under the licence's terms, including the change date.

## What you may not do

- Present a fork or a deployment as **being** Aera, or as endorsed by, affiliated
  with, or operated by Aera Lend.
- Use the name or the mark in your project's name, domain, token symbol, app
  store listing, or social handle in a way a reasonable person would read as
  official.
- Use the mark as your own logo, or a derivative of it as one.

A fork is welcome. A fork wearing this name is somebody else's users being
misled about who holds their money, and that is the only thing this file exists
to prevent.
