# Code of conduct

## The standard

Be straightforward and be decent. Argue about the work, never about the person
doing it.

Specifically, in this project:

* **Disagree with reasons.** "This is wrong because X" is welcome, at any
  volume. "This is bad" is not a review.
* **Be precise about risk.** This is a lending protocol. Overstating a
  vulnerability to win an argument, and understating one to protect a design,
  are both serious.
* **Assume competence.** Someone who has made a mistake is not thereby stupid,
  and the mistake is usually more interesting than the person.
* **Accept being wrong.** Including in public, including after defending a
  position. Nobody here is keeping score.

## Not acceptable

* Harassment, personal attacks, or sustained hostility toward a contributor.
* Discrimination or demeaning comments on the basis of who someone is —
  including age, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or
  expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
  appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.
* Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of a sexual nature.
* Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
* Disclosing a reported vulnerability outside the process in
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
* Using this project's channels to promote a token, a product, or a fork.

## Scope

This applies to every space this project occupies — issues, pull requests,
discussions, commit messages, code comments, and any official channel — and to
anyone representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainers through a private
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/aeralend/aera-v1/security/advisories/new),
which is the private channel available on this repository. Reports are handled
confidentially, and the reporter's identity is not shared with the person
reported.

Maintainers will respond within **5 business days**. In order of severity, the
response may be:

1. A private explanation of why the behaviour was inappropriate.
2. A warning, with conditions on continued participation.
3. A temporary ban from the project's spaces.
4. A permanent ban.

Maintainers who do not follow this in good faith may face consequences decided
by the rest of the maintainers.
